YOUR OPTIONS

It is not one product.
It is four.

Term

Covers you for a set number of years — usually 10, 20 or 30. The most coverage for the lowest monthly cost. It pays your family if you die during the term, and ends when the term does.

Best for: replacing your income while the kids are home and the mortgage is running.

Whole Life

Permanent coverage that never expires. The premium is fixed, the death benefit is guaranteed, and it builds cash value at a guaranteed rate. Costs more per dollar of coverage than term.

Best for: coverage you want in force the day you die, whenever that is.

Indexed Universal Life

Permanent coverage that builds cash value credited based on a market index, with a floor so a down year does not take it. Premiums are flexible, and you can borrow against the cash value while you are alive.

Best for: protection now plus money you can reach later.

Final Expense

A smaller permanent policy, usually $10,000 to $50,000, with simplified questions and no exam. Built to cover a funeral and the bills that follow it.

Best for: older applicants, or anyone who just wants the funeral handled.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The questions
people actually ask.

How much coverage do I need?

A common starting point is ten to twelve times your income, adjusted for the mortgage, any debt, and what it would cost to get the kids through school. We run the number with you on the call rather than guessing at it here.

Do I have to take a medical exam?

Often no. Many carriers now underwrite from data rather than a nurse visit, and a large share of healthy applicants under 60 qualify. Eligibility depends on your age, the amount of coverage, and your history, and some applications still require an exam.

How fast can I be covered?

For applicants who qualify for accelerated underwriting, a decision can come back the same day. Cases that need records or an exam take longer, usually a few weeks. We will tell you which track you are likely on before you apply.

How much coverage can I get?

Coverage is available up to $5,000,000 and higher through some carriers. What you qualify for depends on your income, your age, and underwriting — carriers will not issue an amount far beyond what your income supports.

What does it cost?

Less than nearly everyone guesses. Rates depend on your age, health, the amount, and the type of policy, so a real number requires your actual details. That is most of what the five minutes is for.

What if I have a health condition?

Bring it up early. Carriers price the same condition very differently, and knowing which ones are favorable is a large part of the job. Many conditions that people assume disqualify them do not.

I have coverage through work. Is that enough?

It is a good start and it is rarely enough. Group coverage is usually one or two times salary, and it typically ends when the job does. A policy you own goes with you.

Is this a sales call?

It is a five-minute conversation about what you have, what you need, and what it costs. If term is the right answer, we will say term. If you do not need anything, we will say that too.
